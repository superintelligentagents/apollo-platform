Apollo History Helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The Apollo History Helper is a browser extension used with the Apollo research application (apollo-v2-site.vercel.app), a study that builds long-horizon web-task benchmarks from real browsing.

What the extension does

When you click "Import my Chrome history" on the Apollo web app, the extension reads your recent Chrome browsing history — page URLs, page titles, visit times, and the internal visit/referrer IDs Chrome uses to link a navigation to the page it came from — for roughly the last 120 days, and hands it to the Apollo page open in your browser. It does this only when you ask, and only for the Apollo web app's own pages (an allow-list built into the extension). It communicates with no other website and runs no server of its own; it makes no network requests at all.

Where your data goes

History read by the extension is processed on your device inside the Apollo web page. Nothing is transmitted anywhere until you choose to submit a task. Before entering the Apollo workspace, you must accept a data contribution consent at sign-in. Apollo records the consent version and acceptance time with each submitted task. The review screen still shows the number of selected journeys and visits before submission. When you submit, the task you authored and the selected journeys are uploaded to the study's secure storage. Those journeys include page URLs, titles, visit times, search terms, and navigation/referrer links. History you do not select for a submitted task is never uploaded.

How submitted tasks are used

The tasks you author and submit are research contributions to a benchmark for evaluating AI web agents. The authored task content — your written request, success criteria, difficulty, and the sites involved — may be included in research datasets that are published or licensed to organizations building or evaluating AI agents. You are compensated for this contribution through the study.

Your raw browsing history is never sold, licensed, or published. The visit-level detail attached to a submission is used internally only, to validate the task, and is not part of any published or licensed dataset.

What we do not do

  • We never sell, license, or publish your browsing history.
  • We do not use your history for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • The extension itself sends your data to no server; it only passes it to the Apollo page.

The history permission

The extension requests Chrome's history permission for the single purpose above: letting you import your own history into the Apollo app with one click, instead of manually exporting a file. That is its only function.

Removing it

You can uninstall the extension at any time from chrome://extensions. Removing it stops all history access immediately.

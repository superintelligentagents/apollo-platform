# Odysseys / Apollo expert onboarding

👋 Welcome to the Odysseys data collection. Before starting:

1. Watch the 16-minute [Apollo walkthrough](https://journey-collector-downloads-lj-20241206.s3.amazonaws.com/Odysseys_Apollo_Expert_Onboarding_2026-07-31.mp4).
2. Opedoes n [Apollo V2](https://apollo-v2-site.vercel.app/#/pick-journeys), sign in with your approved email, and complete consent.
3. Create tasks based on real things you would naturally want a web agent to handle. Browsing-history journeys are optional inspiration; you control what is selected and uploaded.

## What makes a strong task

- Natural and genuinely useful, not an artificial trivia exercise.
- Challenging and multi-step, with clear requirements.
- Feasible on the live public web.
- Each step is specific and independently verifiable.
- **Make it work later:** someone should still be able to do the task weeks or months from now. Use timing such as “next week” or “30 days from when the task starts.” Do not include a fixed date, price, schedule, availability, ranking, or answer that will go out of date.
- Do not include sensitive information you are not comfortable sharing.

## Human QC

- Review someone else’s task, never your own.
- Open and verify every step against the live web.
- Edit unclear, duplicated, outdated, or infeasible requirements.
- Approve only when the complete task is clear, useful, feasible, and will still work later.
